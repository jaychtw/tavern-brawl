# Tavern Brawl · frozen design
Approved November 13, 2026. A bilingual, single-screen browser arcade remake inspired by Casio Western Bar. Preserve the approved concept composition in assets/concept.webp. The animated saloon uses a clean plate and individual actors and props.
 
Move one position per press across five brass markers. Fire upward. Break 20 airborne items to begin a showdown. Bottles arc, plates travel farther in time, and heavy mugs drop sooner. Patrons throw at a marked floor position. An apple or outlaw strike costs one of three stars. A missed target costs time. An empty shot resets the bonus. Each hit scores 100 plus 20 per preceding consecutive hit, up to 100 bonus. Lose all stars to end the run. A new run restores three stars.
 
After the twentieth target, cut to an exterior Main Street duel. The sheriff and outlaw stand on the dirt at different depths. Return to the saloon after victory. In the saloon, localize cheering to spectators and keep architecture stationary.

The outlaw targets a marked position, attacks, then gives a return-fire window. Three successful countershots win the duel. Award 100 per countershot and 500 for victory. Subsequent rounds accelerate to bounded speeds. Store the best completed score, language and sound locally.
 
Left/right arrows or A/D move. Space fires. Onscreen controls support touch and keyboard activation. P/Escape pauses. Keep warnings distinguishable by shape and wording, offer mute and reduced motion. English and Traditional Chinese. The title screen uses the approved artwork. Keep game assets local, no paid resources or backend. Deliver a tested feature branch and review PR to jaychtw/tavern-brawl. Merge, Pages release and catalog publication require separate owner direction.
